Cockpit rules

Blank is the safe state.

Line Up hides outdated or invalid data, keeps alerts off the screen during normal flight, and clears the entire display with one button press.

Display rules

  • Clear center view. Approach gates use open corner brackets, and the pitch scale leaves a gap in the center over the runway aim point.
  • Alerts appear only when active. Normal flight states add no extra messages or indicators to the display.
  • Unobstructed instrument panel. A panel keep-out zone clips all head-up symbology off your gauges.
  • One-button blank. One physical press clears all symbology immediately without looking away from the windscreen.
  • Explicit data age and removal. Weather, traffic, and guidance readouts display their age and disappear when they exceed their validity limits.
  • Descriptive audio callouts. Spoken alerts report what was detected and where to look. They never issue flight control instructions.
  • Readback confirmation. Every runway, approach, or route change on the companion app requires confirmation on a readback card before updating the HUD.

Supplemental situational awareness

Line Up is a supplemental situational-awareness system. It is read-only, never connects to flight controls or autopilots, and never replaces certified aircraft instruments, avionics, or official navigation publications. The pilot in command remains responsible for the safe operation of every flight.
